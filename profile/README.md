The internet has become heavier and more demanding than it needs to be, and most of us now carry a collection of
software that quietly works against the person using it. We would like the small part of it we are responsible for to
feel calm, and to be safe in the plain sense of that word, meaning that it holds nothing over you and keeps nothing you
did not offer.

We are drawn to the quiet middle of things, the place where capable software meets someone who should not have to study
it first. Good tools tend to fall down right there, in the setup that nobody explains properly or the failure message
that leaves you guessing. We would happily spend a long season making one narrow thing genuinely good, and we measure a
release by how little it ends up asking of you.

## ✨ How we work

None of this came out of a strategy document. Each product began as something one of us wanted for an evening's own
work, and we stay its heaviest user long after release.

- **It starts as something we needed ourselves.** Both of these began as a tool one of us wanted on an ordinary evening
  and could not find. Nothing here was scoped in a meeting, and that is a good part of why each one stayed narrow enough
  to actually finish.
- **We use them every day, so we notice first.** We are the heaviest users of everything we ship, and we are on it every
  day on our own machines. The annoyance you would have sat down to write to us about has usually already annoyed one of
  us first, which is the cheapest bug report there is.
- **We build on work that already holds up.** Someone has usually spent years solving the difficult part properly, and
  that work deserves to be built upon. We go looking for what is still missing around it, which is almost always the
  part a person actually touches.
- **We keep things close to home.** Your own machine is where we start, and anything living elsewhere has to earn its
  place by making the experience meaningfully better for you. There is a quiet reassurance in knowing that when nothing
  sits in the middle, very little can be taken away from you later.
- **We test against the real thing.** We are wary of tests that only confirm what we already believed. Ours run the real
  software under real conditions, doing the things you would plausibly do with it, because the bugs worth catching have
  a habit of sailing past the easy checks first.

## 🧭 Room we have kept open

We would rather be clear now about the decisions we take one at a time, so that nothing we do later arrives as a
surprise.

- **On price.** Some of what we make costs nothing to use. We weigh that individually for each piece of work, and when
  something is worth paying for we will say so plainly and price it honestly.
- **On openness.** We publish the parts where reading the source is what earns your trust, and we keep others closed. It
  is a judgement we make each time, and we are happy to give our reasons.
- **On accounts.** We ask for as little as a piece of work allows, which so far has meant asking for nothing at all. If
  we build something that genuinely has to know who you are, signing in will be the smallest part of using it, and we
  will hold on to nothing the feature can do without.
- **On services.** Our work has had no need for one so far. Should we build something that genuinely calls for a service
  behind it, we will build it carefully and tell you exactly what it holds and how long it holds it.
- **On scope.** What we have made so far describes where we have been. We expect our interests to move, and we would
  like the freedom to follow them.

## 🔒 What is settled

The section above is where we have deliberately left ourselves room. A page that left itself room everywhere would be
worth very little, so here is the floor underneath it. These four do not move, whatever we go on to build.

- **It's yours, and it stays yours.** Whatever you put into something we make stays yours to read and to take with you,
  and we will not sell it or turn it into a profile of you. We would count it as our own failure if the thing keeping
  you were the awkwardness of going.
- **Nobody buys your attention here.** We will not place advertising inside our work, and we will not take money to put
  somebody else's interest ahead of yours.
- **You can read what it does before you trust it.** Every behaviour worth arguing about is written down in plain
  language, and it sits next to the thing that does it.
- **It keeps working if we stop.** We have no plans to disappear, though it would be careless to write as though nobody
  ever does. Anything we retire keeps working on the machines it already sits on for as long as we can manage, and you
  will hear it from us well before you notice it yourself.
